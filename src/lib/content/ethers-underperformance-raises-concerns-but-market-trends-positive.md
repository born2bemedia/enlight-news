<p>While Ethereum&#8217;s (ETH) price struggles to match Bitcoin&#8217;s 2024 gains, analysts suggest that the cryptocurrency market remains on an upward trajectory.</p>



<p>Ethereum continues to underperform Bitcoin. Despite Bitcoin&#8217;s gains, Ethereum lags, leading to a weaker ETH/BTC ratio. According to Glassnode, the ratio reached its lowest point since April 2021, hitting $0.04622 on May 1.</p>



<p>Glassnode explains that Ethereum&#8217;s underperformance is due to a lag in speculative interest among short-term holders (STH). This cohort comprises investors who acquired their coins within the last 155 days and reflects new investor demand.</p>



<p>While Bitcoin experienced a surge in speculative activity leading up to its all-time high in March, Ethereum has yet to match this trend, as it has not reached its previous all-time high.</p>



<p>The lack of new capital inflows reflects Ethereum&#8217;s underperformance relative to Bitcoin. Glassnode&#8217;s on-chain data reveals that while Bitcoin&#8217;s STH-Realised Cap is almost at the same level as the last bull run peak, Ethereum&#8217;s STH-Realised Cap is less than half of the previous cycle levels, indicating a lack of new capital inflow.</p>



<p>The market is still in the early stages of an uptrend. While Ethereum&#8217;s performance has historically been closely linked to Bitcoin&#8217;s, the recent price action suggests this trend continues.</p>



<p>Both Bitcoin and Ethereum experienced a sell-off after the fourth halving. However, Bitcoin has steadily recovered, consolidating within a specific price range.</p>



<p>As measured from its all-time high, Bitcoin has experienced a deeper correction than Ethereum.</p>



<p>Despite recent challenges, Bitcoin and Ethereum still have a relatively low Realised Cap associated with Long-Term Holders (LTHs), suggesting that the market is still in the early stages of an uptrend.</p>



<p>Capital inflows into Ethereum typically lag behind those into Bitcoin. While Bitcoin has received the majority of inflows, Ethereum has seen weaker speculative activity.</p>



<p>These factors suggest that Ethereum has underperformed relative to Bitcoin, raising investor concerns.</p>
